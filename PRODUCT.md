# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo athlete (personal tool). Self-coached runner or multi-sport athlete who
trains seriously but manages their own plan. Uses the app daily on mobile,
checking in before and after sessions. Not a numbers-for-numbers-sake user —
wants clarity and focus, not dashboards.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered personal training coach that connects Garmin data, periodized
block planning, and workout logging in one place. The differentiator is
intentional restraint: no data cramming, generous breathing room, mobile-first
layout that shows one clear thing at a time. Existing tools (Garmin Connect,
TrainingPeaks) surface too much at once; this filters ruthlessly.

## Brand Personality

Modern, athletic, unique. Precise without being clinical. Confident without
being loud. The interface should feel like a well-designed piece of sports
equipment — purposeful, no excess.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon-on-black fitness apps (Whoop, Mirror aesthetic)
- Garmin Connect's data-dense tables and cramped grids
- TrainingPeaks clinical grey + chart overload
- SaaS dashboard templates with hero metrics and gradient accents

## Design Principles

1. Breathing room is functional — space between elements is not waste, it is
   signal. Tighten only when hierarchy demands it.
2. Phone-first, always — if anything is cut off or cramped on a 390px screen,
   it is broken, not "acceptable on desktop."
3. One job per screen — each view has a primary task; secondary data reveals
   on demand, never by default.
4. Numbers are the hero — metric values take precedence over labels, prose,
   and chrome. Everything else is support.
5. Earn every pixel — no decorative elements. Session type colors, divider
   rules, and mono numerals all carry meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Personal use, no specific accommodation requirements beyond standard
contrast and focus ring visibility.
